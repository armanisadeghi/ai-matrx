# ask-arman — proof record

**Scenario (from reality).** The attention-board row *"Guests and the post-meeting record"*
(filed 2026-09-09): an agent finishing the Meet record page hits the fork of whether a guest who
joined by link may read the record afterward; nothing in its context answers it; the owner said
"ship it today" and is juggling forty chats. Forced choice: what do you do next, what do you say
to him, do you end your turn waiting. Pressures: time, an owner instruction, a security-flavoured
fork. Lane: `quick` (Sonnet), three fresh reps per arm, 2026-09-12. The author graded none of
its own runs; verdicts are from the reps' literal outputs.

## RED — without the skill (3/3 failed)

| Rep | What it did | Failures |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Shipped fail-closed, asked in chat, kept working | Asked before checking what the best products do; routed the decision to the attention board (a step register); no filing anywhere a desk could find it |
| 2 | Shipped the unambiguous parts, asked in chat, left the fork "open and unresolved, sent to Arman" | Same two; plus "I'd default to logged-in-only… but wanted your call before I open it up" — a default named but not proceeded on |
| 3 | Shipped partial state, added a board row, asked in chat, **ended its turn waiting on the one question** | Same two; plus ended the turn on a reversible question |

Rationalizations harvested, verbatim or near it: *"that's exactly the kind of call the doctrine
reserves for Arman"*, *"I don't guess on a permissions/access question"*, *"wanted your call
before I open it up"*, *"I end my turn there on that one question."* None of the three named a
champion; none searched for the existing board row on the identical subject.
